# MDA Colony Master Plan — 12.0m Boulevard Vastu Layout

Interactive CAD Master Plan Explorer for a **29,274.03 m² (38.45 Bighas / 35,011.44 Gaj)** residential township adhering strictly to Meerut Development Authority (MDA) subdivision norms.

---

## Architectural Fixes & Key Highlights

1. **Clear 12.0m (40'-0") Boulevard Corridor:**
   - The 12.0m Main Spine Road (`y = 258 to 298`) is completely clear of plots.
   - Plots North of the road (`y = 8 to 258`) and Plots South of the road (`y = 298 to 368`) are cleanly divided with **zero plot overlap**.
   - Runs uninterrupted from the **East Entrance Gate (`x = 935`)** across the colony till **West Avenue-1 (`x = 47`)**.

2. **Complete 1E–18E Belt Retained:**
   - Col-W1 (North: Plots 1–12; South: Plots 13–15) is fully preserved along the western boundary wall with direct access onto 30'-0" West Avenue-1.

3. **Bottom-Left Corner Void Filled:**
   - The previously empty black corner (`x = 0 to 47`, `y = 368 to 412.46`) is fully integrated into the **West South Row (Plots 46 to 54)**.
   - Every square foot of land is thoughtfully utilized with North-facing plots abutting the 30' Connector Road.

4. **100% Comprehensive Click Inspector:**
   - Clicking **ANY PLOT** displays plot number, facing, exact dimensions in feet/inches and meters, area in Gaj / Sq.Ft / m², setbacks, and abutting road width.
   - Clicking **ANY ROAD** (12m Main Spine, West Avenue-1, West Avenue-2, East Comb Avenues, Connector, or Entrance Gate) displays road classification, length, width, total area, and engineering specifications.
   - Clicking **ANY PARK** (Park-1, Park-2) displays exact dimensions, area share, and community amenities.

5. **0% South-Facing Plots (100% Vastu Compliant):**
   - 78 East-Facing (→)
   - 63 West-Facing (←)
   - 21 North-Facing (↑)
   - **0 South-Facing Plots** (162 total residential plots).

6. **13.00% Parks & Open Greenery (3,805.62 m²):**
   - Strictly meets the mandatory **13% to 15%** MDA quota.

7. **Mobile-First Experience:**
   - Multi-touch pinch-to-zoom and drag-pan.
   - Sliding bottom sheet inspector drawer on tap.
   - Responsive header and thumb-friendly filter chips.
