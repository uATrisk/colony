# MDA Colony Master Plan — 12.0m Boulevard Vastu Layout

Interactive CAD Master Plan Explorer for a **29,274.03 m² (38.45 Bighas / 35,011.44 Gaj)** residential colony development adhering to Meerut Development Authority (MDA) subdivision norms.

---

## Key Highlights

- **12.0m (40'-0") Main Central Spine Road:** Upgraded from 9.0m to 12.0m, extending from the East Entrance Gate all the way across the colony till the **1E–18E belt / West Avenue-1**!
- **Complete 1E–18E Belt Retained:** All 18 plots along the western boundary wall are preserved with direct frontage onto the 30'-0" West Avenue-1.
- **Double-Loaded Access for All Plots:**
  - Plots 1E–18E face **East (→)** onto West Avenue-1.
  - Plots 19W–34W face **West (←)** onto West Avenue-1.
  - Plots 35E–50E face **East (→)** onto West Avenue-2.
  - Plots 51N–56N face **North (↑)** onto the South Connector Road.
  - Plots 57N–68N face **North (↑)** directly onto the **12.0m Main Central Boulevard**!
  - East Blocks 69–164 face strictly **East (→)** or **West (←)**.
- **0% South-Facing Plots:** 100% of the 164 residential plots strictly face **East (82)**, **West (64)**, or **North (18)**. Zero plots face South!
- **13.00% Parks & Open Space (3,805.62 m²):** Exactly meeting the MDA mandatory **13% to 15%** quota.
  - **Park-1 (Central Vastu Community Park):** 3,155.62 m² (10.78%) located north of the 12m Main Spine Road so no plots face South.
  - **Park-2 (Northern Chakroad Green Buffer):** 650.00 m² (2.22%) ecological buffer.
- **No Wasted or Black Voids:** Every sector of the land is thoughtfully planned with road access and plot frontages.
- **Mobile-First Experience:** Multi-touch pinch-to-zoom, touch-pan, bottom-sheet plot inspector drawer, and dark/print modes.
