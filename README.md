## Key Architectural Highlights

1. **Prime Front Commercial Plots (Plots 143 to 158 / COM 1–16):**
   * **Full 52'-0" Prime Commercial Depth:** The front plots (Plots 143 to 158) maintain their full commercial depth (`52'-0"` / 15.85m), providing high-value retail, showroom, and shopfront potential.
   * **Dual Access Advantage:** All 16 commercial plots face the approved 30'-0" Commercial Access Avenue-3 for parking and customer entry, and back directly onto the Grand Front Entrance Gardens.

2. **Park-3: Grand Front Entrance Gardens & Welcome Promenade (827 m² • 3.15%):**
   * **Location:** Directly in the front along the 22'-6" Main Approach Road, flanking both sides of the East Main Gateway (`x = 892` to `934`).

   * **Balanced Park Quota:** Total park area is strictly balanced to **3,707.05 m² (14.11%)**, strictly below the 15% wastage threshold and meeting MDA subdivision rules.

3. **Mobile-First UX & Compact Floating HUD:**
   * **Non-Blocking Mobile Peek Card:** Tapping any plot on mobile displays a sleek floating peek card at the bottom with plot number, commercial/residential designation, dimensions in feet/meters, area in Gaj, facing, and road width without obstructing the map view.
   * **Quick Commercial Filter:** One-tap `⭐ Commercial Frontage (16)` filter instantly highlights the commercial row and pans smoothly to the front sector.
   * **Pinch-to-Zoom & Pan:** Smooth gesture physics with double-tap zoom and zero page-scroll bouncing.

4. **360° Continuous Circulatory Loop (Zero Dead-Ends):**
   * **25'-0" (7.62m) North Loop Road:** Connects the tops of 30'-0" West Avenue-1 and West Avenue-2 across `x = 77` to `x = 165`.
   * Vehicles can drive in from the 12.0m Boulevard, go north up West Avenue-1, loop through the North Loop Road, head south down West Avenue-2, and return directly to the main boulevard.

5. **Continuous Unbroken 1E–18E Belt Along West Wall:**
   * Belt 1 (Plots 1E to 18E) runs unbroken from `y = 8.0` to `y = 412.46` (the southern boundary).
   * Zero empty spaces or gaps. Every plot faces **East (→)** directly onto the 30'-0" wide West Avenue-1.

   * Runs straight and uninterrupted from the **East Entrance Gate Flare (`x = 935`)** across the colony and terminates at **West Avenue-1 (`x = 47`)** in an engineered T-junction.

7. **Correct Diagonal South Boundary (Survey Sheet `IMG_0082`):**
