2. **360° Continuous Circulatory Loop (Zero Dead-Ends):**
   * **25'-0" (7.62m) North Loop Road:** Connects the tops of 30'-0" West Avenue-1 and West Avenue-2 across `x = 77` to `x = 165`.
   * Vehicles can drive in from the 12.0m Boulevard, go north up West Avenue-1, loop through the North Loop Road, head south down West Avenue-2, and return directly to the main boulevard.

3. **Continuous Unbroken 1E–18E Belt Along West Wall:**
   * Belt 1 (Plots 1E to 18E) runs unbroken from `y = 8.0` to `y = 412.46` (the southern boundary).
   * Zero empty spaces or gaps. Every plot faces **East (→)** directly onto the 30'-0" wide West Avenue-1.

4. **Single 12.0m (40'-0") Boulevard Corridor:**
   * Runs straight and uninterrupted from the **East Entrance Gate Flare (`x = 935`)** across the colony and terminates at **West Avenue-1 (`x = 47`)** in an engineered T-junction.
   * Zero plot overlap.

5. **Correct Diagonal South Boundary (Survey Sheet `IMG_0082`):**
   * The boundary step at the south-central edge is an authentic **diagonal slant (`79'-8 1/2"`)** sloping down-and-outward to the East from `(453.96, 333.42)` to `(466.00, 412.46)`.
