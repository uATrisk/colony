# NANDI Township — 2D & 3D Interactive CAD Master Plan

Live Deployment: **[https://uatrisk.github.io/colony/](https://uatrisk.github.io/colony/)**

Interactive CAD & 3D WebGL Master Plan for **NANDI TOWNSHIP** on a **26,274.03 m² (38.45 Bighas / 31,412.06 Gaj / 282,708.5 Sq.Ft)** site adhering strictly to Meerut Development Authority (MDA) subdivision norms.

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Modern Architectural Capabilities

1. **Dual Viewing Modes (2D Vector CAD & 3D WebGL):**
   * **2D Vector Mode:** High-precision SVG CAD drafting canvas with smooth pan, pinch-to-zoom, dynamic visual scale bar, North-up alignment, and real-time CAD dimension lines for selected plots.
   * **3D WebGL Perspective:** Hardware-accelerated 3D model with orbital controls, top-down ortho view, auto-rotate turntable showcase, and battery-efficient on-demand rendering.

2. **Verified 129 Plot Master Plan (100% Vastu Compliant):**
   * **120 Residential Plots:** Standardized ~122 to 130 Gaj plots (`110 Gaj` to `172 Gaj` range).
   * **9 Commercial Plots (C1–C9):** Prime commercial frontage along Avenue 1 near the main arrival gate.
   * **5 Premium Park-Facing Plots:** Expansive ~172 Gaj plots directly overlooking the Central Park.
   * **0% South-Facing Plots:** 62 East-facing (48.1%), 58 West-facing (45.0%), 9 North-facing (7.0%), **0 South-facing (0.0%)**.

3. **Interactive Category & Legend Filters:**
   * One-tap filtering for East-facing, West-facing, North-facing, Premium, Commercial, and Parks.
   * Automatically isolates matching plots with glowing signature colors and dims non-matching plots.

4. **Instant Search & Autocomplete:**
   * Live plot search by number (e.g., `25`, `C1`), category (`prem`, `com`), or facing (`east`, `north`).
   * One-tap selection that smoothly flies the camera to the plot.

5. **CAD Measured 4-Sided Dimensions:**
   * Exact boundary readouts on every plot: Frontage, Rear, Left Depth, and Right Depth.
   * Interactive 2D on-plot CAD dimension ticks.

6. **Sunlight & Vastu Orientation Simulation:**
   * Morning Vastu East sun (casting long dawn shadows), Midday sun, and Golden Hour West sunset illumination.

7. **3D Architectural Villas Toggle:**
   * Interactive 3D contemporary villa models featuring parapets, rooftop decks, and commercial storefronts.

8. **Mobile First Responsive Experience:**
   * Native swipe-down bottom sheet inspector.
   * Touch-friendly controls with safe-area notch support (`100dvh`).
   * Deep linking support (`#plot=25`) with direct WhatsApp & clipboard sharing.
